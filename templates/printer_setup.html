USB or Bluetooth

Open test print
  1. Install the official driver or mobile app for your model.
  2. Connect USB or pair Bluetooth as directed by the model's setup guide.
  3. Print a test page from the driver or app.
  4. Open a bill and choose Thermal receipt (58 or 80 mm), or open a product and choose Print tag.
  5. Use the matching paper size, 100% scale and turn off browser headers/footers.

The website opens your device's print dialog. Bluetooth printers must be supported by the device's print service or the manufacturer's app; pairing alone may not make a printer appear in the browser.
