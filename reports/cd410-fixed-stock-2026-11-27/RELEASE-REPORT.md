# CD410 fixed stock size

The previous tag preview swapped paper width and height when the artwork was turned by 90 or 270 degrees. On wide, short stickers the swapped height could carry the job across multiple labels. A stock measurement now stays fixed at every rotation. If necessary, the app reduces the artwork to fit the available area and displays the reduction. Measured paper width and height can be entered independently of the design and saved in a device profile.

The new 38 × 15 mm preset assumes 38 mm across the web and 15 mm in the direction the paper feeds. For the opposite feed arrangement, enter 15 mm across and 38 mm along the feed under **Paper size, printer alignment and saved profiles**. Remove the backing gap from the measured face height and enter it separately in the driver.

**Printers → Print one test label → Open exact-size test PDF** generates a single PDF page with the selected stock dimensions. It requests actual-size printing and leaves the printer driver settings under the device operator's control. In Windows use the CD410 label driver and the USB printer queue for the first test. Set a custom paper size, choose Gap media, and run Shreyans calibration. A press of Feed should advance exactly one sticker. In the PDF viewer choose Actual size (100%), select the matching paper size, and disable any fit or automatic rotation setting. Stop if the test spans a gap. Recheck the driver or sensor calibration before sending a batch. See [official CD410 support](https://www.shreyanspos.com/pages/driver-for-thermal-label-printer-cd410).

Chromium and WebKit checked rotation, profiles, and grouped tag printing. Chromium print pages retained their stock dimensions, and the server-generated test PDFs were checked for one page at 38 × 15 mm for each rotation plus the opposite stock dimensions. The test PDF renders independently of browser CSS. Physical output on the shop printer is still unverified. This release requires no migration and leaves V1 calculations unchanged.
